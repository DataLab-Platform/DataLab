Open Source Experience 2026
===========================

.. meta::
    :description: DataLab Web presentation at Open Source Experience 2026 in Paris
    :keywords: DataLab, DataLab Web, Open Source Experience, OSXP, 2026, Paris, AI assistant

Pierre Raybaut will present **DataLab Web: open source scientific platform with
a local AI assistant, 100% in-browser** at `Open Source Experience 2026
<https://www.opensource-experience.com/fr/programme-2026>`_.

The talk will include demonstrations of DataLab Web and its AI assistant, as
well as feedback on practical constraints and NLnet funding.

**Event Details**

:Date: December 9, 2026
:Time: 14:00–14:20
:Location: Paris Expo Porte de Versailles, Hall 5.2, Paris, France
:Room: Katherine Johnson
:Track: Open AI, Models, Data and Sovereign Ecosystems
:Format: Talk in French (20 minutes)
