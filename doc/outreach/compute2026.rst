Compute! Paris 2026
===================

.. meta::
    :description: Meet DataLab at the CODRA booth at Compute! Paris 2026
    :keywords: DataLab, Compute! Paris 2026, CODRA, open source, scientific computing, Paris

CODRA will participate in `Compute! Paris 2026
<https://compute.events/paris2026/>`_ as a **Gold Sponsor** and present DataLab
at its booth.

Live demonstrations will cover signal and image processing with examples
involving cameras and detectors, pulses and transients, and radiographic images
for non-destructive testing (NDT).

Visit the booth to try the tools and discuss your experimental data and
scientific software projects with the team.

**Event Details**

:Date: November 25–26, 2026
:Location: Centre International de Conférences de Sorbonne Université, 4 place Jussieu, 75005 Paris, France
